# Types of Networks

## PAN (Personal Area Network)

Range: 1-10 meters

Example:

- Bluetooth
- Mobile Hotspot
- Smart Watch

---

## LAN (Local Area Network)

Range: Building or Office

Example:

- College Lab
- Office Network
- Home WiFi

Advantages

- High Speed
- Low Cost

---

## MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

Range: Entire City

Example

- Cable TV Network
- City Government Network

---

## WAN (Wide Area Network)

Range: Worldwide

Example

- Internet
- AWS Global Network
- Azure Global Network

---

## Difference

PAN < LAN < MAN < WAN
