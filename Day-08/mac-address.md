# MAC Address

A MAC (Media Access Control) Address is a unique physical address assigned to a Network Interface Card (NIC).

Unlike an IP address, a MAC address is generally fixed by the manufacturer.

---

## MAC Address Format

Example:

00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E

It consists of 48 bits (6 bytes).

---

## Types of MAC Addresses

### Unicast

Communication between one sender and one receiver.

---

### Multicast

Communication from one sender to multiple selected devices.

---

### Broadcast

Communication from one sender to all devices on the local network.

Broadcast MAC Address:

FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF

---

## Where is a MAC Address Used?

- Local Area Networks (LAN)
- Ethernet Communication
- Switching
- ARP
