# Hub

A Hub is a basic networking device that connects multiple devices in a Local Area Network (LAN).

It works at the Physical Layer (Layer 1) of the OSI Model.

---

## How Hub Works

- Receives data from one device.
- Broadcasts data to all connected devices.
- Does not check destination address.

---

## Advantages

- Simple to use
- Low cost

---

## Disadvantages

- Creates unnecessary network traffic.
- Less secure.
- Slow compared to switches.

---

## Real-World Example

Old Ethernet networks used hubs to connect computers together.
