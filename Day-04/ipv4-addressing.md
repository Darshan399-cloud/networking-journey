# IPv4 Addressing

An IPv4 address is a unique logical address assigned to every device connected to a network.

IPv4 uses **32 bits** and is divided into **4 octets**.

Example:

192.168.1.10

Each octet ranges from **0 to 255**.

---

## IPv4 Format

192 . 168 . 1 . 10

8 Bits | 8 Bits | 8 Bits | 8 Bits

Total = 32 Bits

---

## Types of IP Address

### Public IP

- Accessible over the Internet
- Assigned by an Internet Service Provider (ISP)

Example:

49.36.120.10

---

### Private IP

Used inside private networks.

Ranges:

10.0.0.0 – 10.255.255.255

172.16.0.0 – 172.31.255.255

192.168.0.0 – 192.168.255.255

---

## Network ID

Identifies the network.

---

## Host ID

Identifies a device within the network.
