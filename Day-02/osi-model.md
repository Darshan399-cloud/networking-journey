# OSI Model (Open Systems Interconnection)

The OSI Model is a conceptual framework that explains how data travels from one computer to another over a network.

It consists of **7 layers**, where each layer performs a specific function.

---

## Why is the OSI Model Important?

- Standardizes network communication.
- Makes troubleshooting easier.
- Helps different devices communicate.
- Used to understand networking concepts.

---
