# UDP (User Datagram Protocol)

UDP is a connectionless protocol.

It sends data without establishing a connection.

UDP is faster than TCP but does not guarantee delivery.

---

## Features of UDP

- Fast Communication
- No Handshake
- No Error Recovery
- Low Overhead

---

## Common UDP Protocols

- DNS
- DHCP
- VoIP
- Online Gaming
- Video Streaming

---

## Difference Between TCP and UDP

TCP

- Reliable
- Slower
- Connection-Oriented
- Ordered Delivery

UDP

- Faster
- Unreliable
- Connectionless
- No Ordered Delivery
